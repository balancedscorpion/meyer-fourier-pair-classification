The source snapshot is `43a4a33069b48d7f303113a3a2ffab25c15f4c8f`, the merge of [PR #1808](https://github.com/ls558/MeyerGeneralProblem/pull/1808). Its native Lean toolchain was 4.34.0-rc1.
